FAQ

Common questions

Is DrawTech Media licensed to fly commercially?

Yes. AJ Drawbaugh holds an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate for small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS), earned in July 2025. That's the certification the FAA requires for commercial drone work.

Who flies the drone and edits the footage?

I do. Every project is personally flown and edited by me, from planning the flight to delivering the final files.

What does a drone shoot cost?

It depends on the type of project, the size of the site and what you need delivered. Send me the details and I'll reply with a quote.

Where do you fly?

DrawTech Media is based in Pennsylvania's Cumberland Valley. If your project is outside the area, reach out and ask.

What if the weather is bad on shoot day?

I only fly when conditions are safe and legal. If wind, rain or visibility rule out a flight, we reschedule.
